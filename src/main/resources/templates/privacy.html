MediSure HIMS Privacy Notice

Version 2026-09

Please read and accept this notice to continue. We need your agreement before you can use the member portal.
You accepted this notice on date.

1. Who we are

This notice explains how the MediSure Lanka Insurance PLC Health Insurance Management System (HIMS) uses your information. It follows the principles of Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, which treats health information as sensitive personal data.

2. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: NIC, full name, date of birth, gender, address, phone number and email.
  • Medical information: blood group, height, weight, allergies, chronic conditions and current medications.
  • Emergency contact details you choose to give us.
  • Family members you cover as dependents, including their medical information.
  • Records of your cover: underwriting applications, policies, premium payments, claims and the documents you upload with them (such as hospital bills), and support tickets.

3. Why we use it

Only to provide your health cover: to assess applications, issue and renew policies, record premiums, process claims, answer your support requests, and keep an audit record that helps prevent errors and fraud. We do not use your information for marketing.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see only your own records.
  • Staff see only what their role needs. Sales agents, underwriters, claims officers and administrators can open your member record. Customer relations staff cannot see your medical information.
  • Every change to your records is recorded in an audit log with who made it and when.

5. How we protect it

Passwords are stored only in hashed form. Access to every page is controlled by role. Claim documents are stored under random file names and shown only to people allowed to see that claim. Your medical details, diagnoses and claim documents are encrypted when stored, and the portal is only available over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

6. Family members you cover

When you add a family member, you confirm that you are allowed to share their details. A dependent aged 18 or over must agree to their own information being recorded. For a child, you consent as their parent or guardian.

7. Your rights

  • See the information we hold about you on your profile page.
  • Correct your personal details by editing your profile.
  • Ask for your information to be deleted, or withdraw your consent, by raising a support ticket. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide your cover.

We keep your records for as long as we need them to administer your cover and to meet our legal record-keeping duties.

8. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will ask you to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in.
